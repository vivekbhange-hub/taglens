# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-21)

First release.

- Reads C2PA Content Credentials: the tool that made or edited the file, each recorded step, the signer, and whether the signature still verifies.
- Reads the IPTC `DigitalSourceType` AI field, and the prompts Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI and InvokeAI save inside PNGs.
- Predicts the label Instagram/Facebook/Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and X are likely to show, from their public policies.
- Shows private data: GPS, camera and lens serial numbers, owner name, timestamps.
- Cleans JPEG, PNG and WebP without re-encoding: metadata blocks are removed and image bytes are copied as they are. Can remove only the location.
- Tool, platform, AI-app and guide pages, all on one engine.

Everything runs in the browser; files are never uploaded.
